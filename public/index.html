Why Now • Mali in Focus

The Talent Is Ready. We Build the Path From Zero Digital Skills to Paid AI Work.

In September 2026, Mali opened the new site of its national AI and robotics center, CIAR-Mali, and the government announced an annual national AI forum for youth. The talent is ready. What is missing is a practical path from no digital skills to paid AI work. BAARA — the AI Institute of Mali — builds that path in Bamako.

  • Start Where You Are: Learners can stop after any track with a job-ready skill, or pass the exit gate and continue.
  • 70% Hands-On Project Work: Taught in French with Bambara support using real Malian market, rainfall, and clinic datasets.
  • 50% Target Share of Women Learners: Supported by learner stipends, transport allowances, and shared solar-backed laptops.

The Program • 4 Stackable Tracks

Four Tracks. Each One Ends in Paid Work.

Learners start where they are. They can exit into employment after any track with a verified portfolio, or pass the exit gate to advance to the next track.

Track 1 • 8 Weeks • Start Here

Use a laptop, spreadsheets, and real local data: market prices, rainfall, and clinic visits. Builds foundational digital fluency from zero prior computer experience. ↳ Exit to: Data Entry, Office Assistant

Digital & Data Foundations

Track 2 • 6 Weeks • Then

Write structured prompts, audit AI output for errors and hallucinations, and use AI workflows for business reports, French/Bambara translation, and customer messages. ↳ Exit to: AI-Assisted Admin, Content, Support

Applied AI for Work

Track 3 • 16 Weeks • Go Deeper

Master Python, data analysis, Git, and machine learning engineering — culminating in a supervised capstone model built and evaluated on a real Malian dataset. ↳ Exit to: Junior Data Analyst, Junior ML Developer

Python & Machine Learning

Track 4 • 24-Week Fellowship

Collect Bambara speech and text, train speech recognition (ASR) and translation models, and publish open datasets and weights under public licenses with contributor credit. ↳ Exit to: NLP Engineer, Research Assistant

Bambara NLP Lab

Why a Bambara Lab • Open Malian AI

Inside the Bambara NLP Lab: AI in the Language Most Malians Speak Every Day

Most AI tools do not work well in Bambara. Our 24-week fellowship builds on open work by Malian researchers: the MALIBA-AI speech benchmark and the Bayelemabaga dataset of 47,000 Bambara–French sentence pairs. Every dataset and model is released openly under public licenses, with credit to each Malian contributor.

First Use Case 01 — Maternal & Community Health:

Verified maternal health & clinic triage information in spoken Bambara

Fellows train speech recognition and verified medical translation models so expectant mothers and community health workers can ask questions and hear accurate guidance in Bambara.

Fund This Lab Track
First Use Case 02 — Voice Farm Advice:

Spoken Bambara weather, soil & market price advisory for rural farmers

Built on MALIBA-AI and Bayelemabaga (47,000 Bambara–French sentence pairs), delivering voice-first agricultural advice, rainfall alerts, and local market prices over low-bandwidth mobile links.

Partner on Open Datasets
First Use Case 03 — Civic Access by Voice:

Government & administrative forms read aloud in Bambara

Bridging the literacy divide by reading official French administrative forms aloud in natural Bambara and helping citizens complete essential public records accurately.

HOW WE OPERATE • CAMPUS INFRASTRUCTURE

Engineered for Bamako. Designed for Uninterrupted Learning.

Reliable power and fast local access to datasets, coding tools, and AI models are essential for daily hands-on engineering. Our Bamako campus is engineered from the ground up to keep classes, coding labs, and model training running smoothly every day.

300

Learners / Year from 2028 (88 in 2027 Pilot)

1. Solar-First Campus Power

Solar panels and battery banks serve as our primary campus power source, keeping classrooms, servers, and shared laptops running continuously.

2. Offline-Ready Local Servers & Regional Sync

On-campus servers cache video courses, Python libraries, documentation, and compact AI models for instant local access, backed by regional cloud synchronization.
